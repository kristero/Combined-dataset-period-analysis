# MPC rotation-period pipeline (Nagainis, Eglitis & Zarins)

Analysis code of the paper *Through the Forest of Aliases: New, Reliable Asteroid Rotation Periods from Minor Planet
Center Photometry*. The scripts locate their inputs relative to their own file, so this folder keeps the layout of the
working directory. Large inputs and outputs (MPC downloads, Excel workbooks, result arrays, DAMIT runs) are not
included; they are regenerated by the scripts below.

## Environment

- Period pipeline: CPython 3.12.7, Astropy 6.1.3, NumPy 1.26.4, SciPy 1.13.1, pandas 2.2.2, Matplotlib 3.9.2,
  lmfit 1.3.4, sbpy 0.6.0, scikit-learn 1.5.1, astroquery 0.4.11. `hpc/setup_py312.sh` builds the same environment on
  a Linux cluster.
- `repo_snap/` holds the analysis classes as used for the paper: `Asteroid_pickle.py` (version of 2026-09-16) and
  `asteroid_ls.pyc` (CPython 3.12 bytecode of `asteroid_ls.py` of 2026-09-16, whose source was not committed).
  `compare_ls_source.py` shows that `asteroid_ls.py` of commit 5a7642a of this repository compiles to identical code
  objects except for two plotting methods (`visualize_ls`, `visualize_time_series_all_oppositions`) that gained
  optional display arguments.
- Convex inversion: DAMIT `convexinv` / `period_scan` version 0.2.1 (`damit_*.py`).

## Chain

1. `mpc_fetch.py` – MPC observations (ADES) and JPL Horizons geometry; workbooks per asteroid.
2. `zp_split.py` (`testswap_split.py` for asteroids added later) – 703/G96 zero-point split at 2018 July 1.
3. `mpc_redo.py` – screening, HG1G2 combination, Lomb–Scargle search, candidates, subsets (zp tree: `zp_setup.py`).
4. `rebootstrap.py` – night-block bootstrap of the candidates.
5. `adjudicate_pre.py` – algorithmic proposal and evidence summary per asteroid; review verdicts in
   `review_results.json`; `finalize.py` merges them into `adopted.json`.
6. `rebootstrap2.py`, `quality_rule.py` – bootstrap and subset tests of the final candidates, quality codes
   (`quality_final.json`).
7. `geometry_validate.py`, `geometry_reanalysis.py`, `refine_periods.py` – aspect model and corrected-data analysis.
8. `amp_*.py` – amplitudes per apparition, injection tests.
9. `damit_prep.py`, `damit_run.py`, `damit_mirrors.py`, `damit_ellipsoid.py`, `damit_eval.py`, `damit_paper_table.py`
   – convex inversion and acceptance; `review_checks.py`, `review_inertia2.py` – inertia and other checks.
10. `ls_longperiod.py`, `longperiod_tests.py`, `damit_longscan.py`, `damit_longrange.py` – long-period tests.
11. `make_figures.py`, `fig_dataset_matrix.py`, `dataset_power_table.py`, `damit_shapes_page.py`, `make_tables.py`
    – figures and tables. `review_*.py` – analyses added in the revision (blind rerun, reliability columns,
    candidate test asteroids, LCDB/DAMIT look-ups).

SLURM job scripts for the LU HPC are in `hpc/`.

## Revised test sample (2026-10-26)

Five test asteroids that earlier papers had already used ((1951), (1963), (2134), (2150), (2318); `retired.json`) were
replaced by (249), (486), (870), (1600), (1700) and (7750), analysed blind. `adopted_retired.json`
(`testswap_retired_adopted.py`) keeps the pre-swap entries of the retired asteroids: (1963) and (2134) keep their convex
models (`convex_only`), and (2150) remains an injection template.

- `testswap_sbdb.py` (SBDB records without the rotation period until `--unblind`), `testswap_split.py`,
  `testswap_register.py` (review verdicts, `published.json`, `finalize.py`), `testswap_repro_check.py` (Python 3.12
  environment on the HPC against the local run).
- HPC jobs `hpc/testswap_period.sbatch`, `testswap_geo.sbatch`, `testswap_damit.sbatch`, `testswap_post.sbatch`,
  `testswap_post2.sbatch`, `testswap_final1.sbatch`, `testswap_final1b.sbatch`, `testswap_final2.sbatch`.
- `testswap_checks.py` repeats the sample-dependent checks of `review_checks.py` (harmonic test, principal axis,
  phase function, DAMIT membership). `damit_rejected.json` lists the models rejected by the principal-axis check,
  which `damit_paper_table.py` keeps out of the table and the shape figure. `review_870_alt.py` fits a convex model of
  (870) at its competing period.
- `testswap_compare_old.py` compares the outputs with the pre-swap copies, `testswap_litcompare.py` compares the test
  periods with the literature (also in units of P^2/P_orb), and `testswap_numbers.py` recomputes every sample-dependent
  number of the manuscript (`testswap_numbers.json`).
- Pole and sidereal-period comparisons (`damit_eval.py`) use only published solutions whose sidereal period agrees with
  ours within 0.5 %.
- `manto_report.py` makes the diagnostic figures of the (870) failure (`review/manto/`, `manto_numbers.json`).

## Checks suggested by the (870) failure (2026-10-27, not applied to the grades)

- Blind validation run: `vb_setup.py` builds `vb/` (the 120 validation asteroids, LCDB periods removed from the SBDB
  copy), `hpc/vb_blind.sbatch` runs the period search, bootstrap, algorithmic proposal (`vb_autofinal.py`, no review),
  subset tests and quality rule. `vb_evaluate.py` compares the blind periods with the LCDB periods by grade and applies
  the dominant-dataset rule to the validation and paper samples (`vb_evaluate.json`).
- Inertia check: `inertia_validation.py` (validation models at their LCDB periods), `review_alt_period.py` (models at
  the competing periods of (3716) and (4239); `--collect` rebuilds the summary from finished runs),
  `inertia_sensitivity.py` (validation models at the strongest wrong candidate of the blind run).
- Synodic-sidereal consistency: `synsid_check.py` (`synsid_check.json`).
